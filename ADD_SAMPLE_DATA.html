    How this project works

    The full pipeline, from raw data to a prediction.

    1

    Extract

    Property data is fetched from the data source (01_EXTRACT.PY).

    2

    Transform

    Pandas removes duplicates, missing values and invalid records.

    3

    Validate & Load

    Pydantic checks every record against the rules, then the data is saved to a SQLite database.

    4

    Train & Serve

    A Gradient Boosting model is trained and served through the FastAPI /predict endpoint.

    Frequently asked questions

    How many sq ft is 1 Kanal?

    1 Kanal = 20 Marla = 5,445 sq ft. 1 Marla = 272.25 sq ft.

    How is the likely range calculated?

    Two extra models are trained to predict the 10th and 90th percentile prices, so the real price should fall between them about 80% of the time.

    What does “Demo mode” mean?

    When the backend server is unreachable, the page falls back to a simple built-in formula. “Live model” means the answer came from the trained model.

    Is this the real market price?

    It is only an estimate. The actual price can differ depending on the street, the condition of the house and the market.
